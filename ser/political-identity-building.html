Political Identity Building™

Building authentic political brands through research, narrative strategy, media communication and long-term public engagement.

What It Is

A political identity built beyond polling day.

UB's flagship, always-on service, run in two connected phases rather than sold as a single static package.


Campaign-phase PIB makes a candidate genuinely appealing and rationally legible to voters of every age: message-house development, press strategy, rapid-response media handling, spokesperson training and reputation management.


In-Office PIB continues after the result: a standing accountability and transparency programme that keeps the winning candidate answerable to the electorate for the full term, not just for the campaign.

What We Deliver

Built around both phases of political identity.

Deliverables — Campaign Phase

  • Messaging framework
  • Media training day
  • Monthly press office support
  • Crisis comms retainer

Deliverables — In-Office Phase

  • Quarterly Promises Tracker report (campaign commitments vs delivery)
  • A public town-hall/Q&A calendar
  • Constituent-communication templates
  • A light ongoing press-office watching brief
Ideal Client

Designed for political leaders across the electoral journey.

Councillors, PPCs, mayoral candidates, backbench MPs, small party regional offices — and, post-election, the same client renewing into the in-office phase.

UB's Founding Thesis

Political identity is more than perception on polling day.

PIB is deliberately the service most closely tied to UB's founding thesis: political identity is not just how a candidate is perceived on polling day, it is how a representative remains accountable and transparent to voters of all ages for the whole of their term.
