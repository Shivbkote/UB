The story behind United Ballots.

Letter From The Founder & CEO

Hi there,

"Leadership begins long before election day."

Why do people place their trust in one leader over another? Was it policy? Personality? Party loyalty? Or something far more human? That curiosity eventually introduced me to a field I instantly fell in love with - psephology, the study of elections and voting behaviour.

While many people follow elections for the headlines, I found myself fascinated by the numbers behind them. I wasn't just interested in ‘who’ won. I wanted to understand ‘why’ they won. That curiosity led me to begin my professional journey as a “Political Consultant Trainee” in the world's largest democracy - India.

If you've ever experienced an Indian election, you'll know it is impossible to forget. It's loud, colourful, chaotic, emotional, data-driven and, occasionally, powered by more cups of chai than anyone should reasonably consume.

I collected booth-level voting patterns, campaign observations and far too many notebooks filled with electoral insights. It was there that I learnt one lesson that has stayed with me ever since:
People rarely vote for the most qualified candidate. They vote for the candidate they understand and trust.

That lesson became the foundation of everything I would later build. My journey then brought me to London, where studying International Relations at the London School of Economics and Political Science (LSE) gave me the opportunity to explore British democracy through a different lens. Here, I discovered a political culture rooted in strong institutions, local representation and one of the world's oldest parliamentary traditions.
Beyond the classroom, I immersed myself in British politics - not as a spectator, but as a researcher and practitioner. Working on a successful council election campaign in the London Borough of Hillingdon reinforced a belief that transcends borders:
The principles of electoral success may be universal, but every democracy has its own political language.

And, Britain is no exception. Its electoral landscape is shaped by local issues, constituency dynamics, community trust and disciplined communication. Winning here is not about borrowing strategies from elsewhere; it is about understanding the unique rhythm of British democracy and helping candidates build genuine relationships with the people they seek to represent.
United Ballots was born from that belief. Not to manufacture political personalities. Not to script politicians. But to help public leaders discover, define and communicate an identity that people genuinely believe in. Whether you're standing for your first council election, contesting a parliamentary seat or serving an established community, every campaign begins with the same question:

“Why should people trust you?”
Finding that answer is where our work begins.


Thank you for visiting The UB website! I hope this is the beginning of a meaningful conversation.

Warm regards,
Masha Tokas
Founder & CEO
