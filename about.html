Political identity is more than a campaign.

Our Philosophy

Every victory begins with an identity.

Politics has never lacked ideas. It has lacked connection.

At United Ballots, we believe people vote for people before they vote for policies. That means political leaders must be able to communicate who they are, what they stand for and why their leadership matters.

We combine research, strategy and storytelling to help political leaders build identities that resonate with voters and endure beyond election day.
